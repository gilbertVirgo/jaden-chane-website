FAQs

Questions

Can I bring a plus-one?

We'd love to celebrate with everyone, but we're keeping things intimate, so we can't accommodate plus-ones. Your invitation names everyone we've invited.

Are children welcome?

If your invitation is addressed to your family (“The … family”), your children are invited too — we can't wait to see them.

Is there an area for kids?

Yes. There's a jungle gym at the venue and a kids' area by the chapel, and under-7s will have their own little table at dinner. We're also looking into a babysitter for the day — more on that soon.

What time should I arrive?

From 16:00, so you have time to park, grab a drink and find a seat before the ceremony starts at 16:30. The full day is in our schedule.

Is there parking?

Yes — there's a large grassed area on your right as you drive into the venue, with plenty of space for everyone.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Unfortunately not. We'd really love you to be present with us during the ceremony, so our photographer will capture every moment. Once the reception starts, snap away — and add your favourites to our shared album.

Will I need a coat?

It's February, so almost certainly not — expect a warm summer evening. Leave the coat at home and bring your dancing shoes.

What happens if it rains?

There's a lovely chapel on site, so if the weather isn't on our side we'll simply move inside. Nothing you need to plan for.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes, it is. If there's anything that would make the day easier for you, just let us know on your RSVP.

What time does the evening finish?

The party winds down at [00:00] — see the schedule for the full evening.

Will you join us?

Kindly reply by [RSVP deadline]. Let us know about any dietary needs while you're there.
